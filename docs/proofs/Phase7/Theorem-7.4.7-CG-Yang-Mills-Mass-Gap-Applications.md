## §8. Physical Interpretation and Predictions

### §8.1 Physical Content of the Mass Gap

The mass gap $m > 0$ in pure SU(3) Yang-Mills theory means:

1. **No massless gluons:** Free gluons do not exist as asymptotic states. The lightest state is a **glueball** — a color-singlet bound state of gluons — with mass $m_{0^{++}} \approx 1.5-1.7$ GeV.

2. **Confinement:** Color-charged states are confined. The spectrum contains only color-singlet states with a gap above the vacuum.

3. **Exponential decay of forces:** The gluon-mediated force between static color charges decays exponentially at distances $r \gg 1/m$:
$$V(r) \sim \sigma r - \frac{\alpha_s}{r} + \text{const} \qquad (r \lesssim 1/m)$$
$$V(r) \to \text{const} + O(e^{-mr}) \qquad (r \gg 1/m)$$

4. **Unique vacuum:** The cluster property (OS4) ensures the vacuum is unique — there is no spontaneous symmetry breaking in pure Yang-Mills theory.

### §8.3 String Tension and Confinement

The string tension $\sigma$ measures the energy per unit length of the chromoelectric flux tube between static quarks. In the CG framework:

$$\sqrt{\sigma}_\text{CG} = \frac{\hbar c}{R_\text{stella}} = \frac{197.327 \text{ MeV·fm}}{0.44847 \text{ fm}} = 440 \text{ MeV}$$

$$\sigma_\text{CG} = (440 \text{ MeV})^2 = 0.194 \text{ GeV}^2$$

On the FCC lattice, the exact string tension is:

$$\sigma_\text{lat}(\beta) = -\ln u_\mathbf{3}(\beta)$$

with the remarkable property that this is the strong-coupling result for **all** $\beta < \beta_c$ (Prop 7.4.4a) — no non-perturbative corrections exist on the FCC lattice.

### §8.4 Connection to Phenomenology (Hadronic Physics)

In full QCD (with dynamical quarks), the mass gap takes on a richer meaning:

1. **Glueball mixing:** The $0^{++}$ glueball mixes with $q\bar{q}$ scalars ($f_0(500)$, $f_0(980)$, $f_0(1370)$, $f_0(1500)$, $f_0(1710)$). The CG prediction $m_{0^{++}} \approx 1.5$ GeV is in the range of $f_0(1500)$ and $f_0(1710)$, the primary glueball candidates.

2. **Pion mass:** The pion ($m_\pi = 135$ MeV) is much lighter than the glueball because it is a pseudo-Goldstone boson of chiral symmetry breaking, not a mass gap state. The mass gap in the full theory is still set by the glueball scale.

3. **Proton mass:** The proton mass ($m_p = 938$ MeV) is mostly generated by QCD dynamics (not quark masses). The CG framework's mass gap scale $m_{0^{++}} \approx 1.5$ GeV is consistent with the proton being a bound state at a fraction of the glueball mass.

### §8.7 Future Directions: Beyond Pure Gauge

The CG Yang-Mills mass gap program addresses pure SU(3) gauge theory. Extensions include:

1. **Dynamical quarks:** Coupling fermion fields to the FCC lattice gauge theory. The CG framework's phase-gradient mass generation mechanism (Phase 3) provides a candidate for quark mass generation without the standard Higgs mechanism.
